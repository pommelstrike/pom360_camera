# Bill of Materials — RV Gearbox

Bearings + fasteners for the 3D-printed RV (planetary–cycloidal) reducer. These are
**motor-independent** — adapting to a NEMA 17 removes none of them. Printed parts are in
[../STL/](../STL); part dimensions are in [technical-description.md](technical-description.md).
Structured data: [../bom/hardware.csv](../bom/hardware.csv).

> Tip: an **M3 assortment box** (×6–×25) and an **M5 pack** cover all the screw lengths in one buy.
> Bearings come cheapest in 10-packs.

### Bearing roles

- **45×58×7 (×1)** — main output-hub bearing.
- **15×24×5 (×9)** — cycloidal roller/pin bearings.
- **20×27×4 (×3)** — seat the three eccentric cams.

for the build order.
